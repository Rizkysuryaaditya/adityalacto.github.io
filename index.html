How I run a campaign

01

Audit

Check the funnel, the competitors, the tracking and where the budget leaks. I won't spend a rupiah on ads I can't measure.

02

Plan

Agree a ROAS or cost target and a budget split before anything goes live.

03

Launch

Ads go live, with creatives made together with designers and content creators.

04

Optimize

Small A/B tests on creatives, headlines and audiences first, then scale only what works.
